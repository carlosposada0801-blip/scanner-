# Bookmark Club

An original, nostalgic reading-rewards site in the style of late-2000s kids' learning websites.

- **Read**: 6 original stories in 3 levels, with a read-along timer, words-per-minute, a "Read it to me" voice, and a 3-question quiz.
- **Earn ★ tokens**: for reading, right answers, perfect scores, the daily bonus and word games.
- **Catalog**: spend tokens on furniture, toys, wall decorations and wallpapers.
- **Clubhouse**: decorate your own room with everything you've bought, with your Bookmark Buddy.

No install needed: open `index.html` in a browser. Progress saves in the browser (localStorage).
Add more stories or prizes by editing `data.js`.
