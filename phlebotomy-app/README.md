# Phlebotomy Practice

A study and practice app for phlebotomy class, built as a single file. Open `index.html` in any phone or computer browser. It needs no install or internet connection after it loads. Progress is saved on your device.

**Add it to your phone's home screen:** open the page in Safari or Chrome, tap Share (or the ⋮ menu), then tap **Add to Home Screen**.

## What's inside
- **Learn:** order of draw and every tube (additive, tests, inversions), butterfly needles, gauges and equipment, venipuncture steps, vein/site selection, capillary and heel sticks, special handling, complications, safety and infection control, anatomy and terms
- **Practice:**
  - Order of Draw game (basic 7 tubes, or advanced 11 tubes)
  - Requisition drill: read the test orders, then pick the right tubes in the right order
  - Name That Tube
  - Procedure-steps sequencing
  - Quiz by topic (104 questions with explanations)
  - Flashcards (54)
  - 25-question mock exam
  - Review of questions you missed
- **Progress:** accuracy by topic and the topics you're weakest in

This is a study aid only. Always follow your instructor, textbook, and facility policy, because tube colors and some procedures vary by manufacturer and facility.
